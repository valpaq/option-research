# Reproduce the Rysk expiry cohort

The fixed window is 10 October 2025 08:00 UTC through 9 October 2026
08:00 UTC, inclusive at both ends (365 calendar dates; 364 elapsed days). It selects expiry dates,
not writing dates or redemption transaction timestamps.

Use Python 3.14, install this bundle's `requirements-lock.txt`, and install the
repository library with `python -m pip install --no-deps -e .`. Installing the
dependencies can require network access. The analysis itself is offline.

Set the working directory to `research/rysk/rysk-year`, then open
`notebook.ipynb` and select **Restart Kernel and Run All**, or use the
`NotebookClient` command in the public repository README. The notebook then:

1. Verifies the input and source-code SHA-256 hashes, then unpacks an isolated
   working copy of the source data.
2. Resolves collateral quantities from token-specific transfers for the included
   receipt subset. Remaining rows use source ledger quantities. The separate
   5,000 kHYPE transfer and 50-unit mint are checked below.
3. Selects the inclusive expiry cohort, reconstructs each sale and prices its
   payoff using the protocol's expiry oracle. Missing eligible rows or prices
   stop execution.
4. Calculates option P&L and the combined collateral scenario, aggregates by
   wallet, underlying and expiry, and independently reconciles with decimal
   arithmetic.
5. Prints the canonical metrics and accounting identities, exports the row and
   wallet tables, and draws every PNG and SVG. The article uses nine of the
   nineteen notebook figures.

`figures.json` is generated by the notebook. `tables/settled.csv` exposes each
trade's option result and collateral scenario. `tables/wallets.csv` aggregates the same components
by address. `tables/accounting-audit.json` contains the independent comparison;
`tables/collateral-reconstruction.json` identifies the receipt logs used for
collateral quantities. `tables/collateral-exposure-anomalies.json` records
the oversized transfer and its sensitivity. The notebook checks every other
call against its contract quantity and every put against strike notional.

The [5,000 kHYPE transfer and 50-option mint](https://hyperevmscan.io/tx/0xf88a8ca30a002e4d59ceaf0f6bab9a90db035edf240cada894f3361d5e882403)
occurred at HyperEVM block 22,529,167. Its signed payload contains
`collateralAmount` = 5,000 kHYPE and `quantity` = 50 option units. The
[implementation executed in that transaction](https://hyperevmscan.io/address/0xfad2adf5aca6043cc00def6e74ef901725d14632/advanced)
deposits the former and mints the latter. The pinned payload and receipt are
included in `data/`; the notebook checks those fields against the transfer
and mint logs. It retains 5,000 collateral units in the main scenario and
reports the 50-unit counterfactual separately.

The provenance manifest records the latest incremental sales scan's terminal block and hash,
collection timestamp, dependencies and exact analysis-code hashes. Input
hashes apply to decompressed bytes and are listed in `data/CHECKSUMS.txt`.

## Sources and interpretation

The sales snapshot is derived from chain logs and transaction receipts.
The cohort covers primary mints/writes, not secondary transfers. Candidate
transactions without a mint or with ambiguous multi-series mints are excluded.
In a Rysk primary write, the writer is the liquidity taker: MMarket receives
the minted option and pays the writer's premium. The opposite sign of the
writer's option result describes the initial long-option side if it held each
option to expiry. This analysis does not trace later ownership or reconcile
redemption transfers, so it is not a measured MMarket trading P&L.
The provenance manifest records incremental scan boundaries and an overlapping
receipt-count replay range; it is not a complete re-scan of the older ledger.
The included collateral receipt evidence
is a targeted subset of the sales snapshot; it is not a full receipt archive
for every sale. Option payoff is oracle-derived intrinsic value; actual
redemption transfers are not independently reconciled.

The legacy ledger column `fee` is maker-side stablecoin paid to addresses
other than the writer. A full-cohort receipt scan matched all 40,067 sales
against the ledger: $1,872,581.799852 went outside writer wallets, including
$1,872,560.358196 to the address observed as Rysk's `feeRecipient` on
27 September 2026. The notebook checks the recipient split against the ledger
and pinned receipts for the less-common transfers. The full receipt set is not
bundled; the scan summary records its transaction-set hash and recipient totals.
The [RyskHype read-as-proxy contract](https://hyperevmscan.io/address/0x8c8bcb6d2c0e31c5789253ecc8431ca6209b4e35#readProxyContract)
reported `feeRecipient = 0xfb69f38eae27705720eb4aabb04be9edbec5b555`,
`gasFeeRecipient = 0xafe32eb89391dfd5900f98857f009477e4423db4`, and
`mmarket = 0x691a5fc3a81a144e36c6c4fbca1fc82843c80d0d` on 2026-09-27.
The pinned scan records these method results and their selectors. These labels
were observed on 27 September: earlier or later recipient settings were not
reconstructed. The receipts establish the amounts sent during the study window.

Spot at writing uses cached Hyperliquid hourly/four-hourly candle closes,
with pinned Binance minute observations near the strike when available
(HYPE futures; other supported assets use spot). Candle closes are price
proxies rather than execution quotes.

The combined scenario prices each collateral unit as one unit of the
underlying. kHYPE, LHYPE, wstHYPE and PT-kHYPE exchange rates, staking accrual,
discounts and wrapper basis are not measured. The wallet and expiry estimates
therefore do not establish token-specific collateral returns or wallet-wide
investment P&L. Stablecoin collateral is held at face value. Gas, financing,
hedges, external holdings and movement between successive trades are excluded.

Strike distance means strike divided by spot at writing, minus one. Monetary
collateral totals sum individual trade lifetimes; reused capital is not TVL.
